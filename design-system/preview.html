Design system · v1

Sage & Paper

Calm paper and sage for long sessions, warm clay for wasted time, and one handwritten voice for your own words. Every token below is a CSS variable in tokens.css. Toggle the theme from the top bar to check both modes.

Principles

Calm by default

Low-contrast paper surfaces and soft sage. Colour is saved for meaning, so a red number always matters.

Honest colour

Clay always means wasted time. Hatched grey always means unknown. Planned fun is pale clay, not wasted.

One window

The page never scrolls. Panels scroll inside themselves. Everything fits from 1366×768 up to 2560×1440.

One tap first

The most likely action is the biggest, closest button. Keyboard shortcuts for everything you do daily.

Your handwriting

The handwritten font is only for words you wrote yourself: your daily goal, what you learned. Never for system text.

Colour

Sage is the brand and the colour of study. Surfaces are warm paper, never pure white or black.

Sage scale

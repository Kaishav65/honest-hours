# Honest Hours

- **Start of every session:** read [TODO.md](TODO.md) (progress + next step), then the relevant sections of [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).
- **When a step is finished:** tick it in TODO.md, update "Current status" and "Last updated".
- **After every task/step:** record it under that step in TODO.md (Done + files, Decisions = options considered and which was chosen and why, Verified, Not verified / known limits; see "How to record a step" under v2). TODO.md is the project history: never delete old records.
- Never print or commit `.env.local` (contains the database password).
